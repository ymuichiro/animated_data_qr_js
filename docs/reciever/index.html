Scanning tips

Preset awareness

Receiver help

How to scan successfully

  1. Press Start camera and scan and allow the camera permission prompt.
  2. Point the camera at the sender display so the full QR area stays inside the frame.
  3. Hold steady while the receiver reads the manifest and collects chunks.
  4. Wait for the progress bar to reach 100%, then download the reconstructed file.

If the transfer keeps missing frames, move closer, reduce reflections, or ask the sender to use a more conservative preset.

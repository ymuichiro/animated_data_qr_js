How to scan successfully

  1. Open the scan stage when the sender is ready.
  2. Allow camera access as soon as the scan stage opens.
  3. Keep the sender QR large enough in the frame that the code stays crisp and easy to focus.
  4. Watch the live progress in the modal until the main page shows the save or download action.
  5. Folder transfers can be saved directly as extracted files or downloaded as a standard ZIP fallback.

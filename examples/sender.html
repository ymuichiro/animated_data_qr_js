Sender help

How to run the sender

  1. Select the file you want to share.
  2. Choose a preset based on your environment. The preset automatically sets density, speed, and recovery.
  3. Press Prepare transfer to generate the animated QR loop.
  4. Once the receiver is already scanning, press Start broadcast.
  5. Keep the sender bright, centered, and stable until the receiver reports completion.
